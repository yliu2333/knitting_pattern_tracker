SAMPLE BEANIE (one size)
Gauge: 20 sts and 28 rows = 10 cm in stockinette on 4.5 mm needles.

BRIM
With 4 mm circular needle, long-tail cast on 96 sts. Place marker for beginning of round and join, being careful not to twist.
Rib round: *k1, p1; rep from * to end.
Repeat rib round 11 more times (12 rounds total).

BODY
Change to 4.5 mm needle.
Knit every round until hat measures 15 cm from cast-on edge.
Set-up round: *k12, pm; rep from * 6 more times, k12.

CROWN
Round 1 (decrease): *knit to 2 sts before marker, k2tog, sm; rep from * to end.
Round 2: knit.
Work rounds 1–2 a total of 6 times.
Final round: *k2tog; rep from * to end.

FINISHING
Cut yarn leaving a 20 cm tail, thread through remaining stitches and pull tight.
